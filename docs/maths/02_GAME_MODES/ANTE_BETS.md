# Ante Bet

**ANT-001 -** Ante Bet is an adapation of config to force an increase of free game entries.
**ANT-002 -** Usually there will not be a engine change to require Ante Bet functionality. Nor a specific PAR setting outside set selection and symbol insertion. 
**ANT-003 -** There will be config in PAR for Ante Bets own reels , insertion tables.
**ANT-004 -** Bonus buys have a cost associated with them , normally around 2x of base stake.
**ANT-005 -** Wins are still awarded by base stake.
