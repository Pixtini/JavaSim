# Win caps

Define win caps.

**WIN-001 -** The win cap is the maximun payout by multiplier on any paid round. 
**WIN-002 -** Both basegame + freegame is taken into account for the the cap.
**WIN-003 -** If triggered with respins, cascades, freespins or features left to maintain the round ends win capped at the set cap and player awarded that amount.
**WIN-004 -** A win message is normal to announce win cap has been reached.

# Examples

Example of WIN-001/WIN-002, Win cap is set at 500x base bet, the player has been given 50x in basegame and awarded freegame , their maximun entitlement from freegames is now 450x. 

Example of WIN-003, the player hits that 450x entitlement on spin 3/5, the round is ended, and balance of 500x is paid, the player is then elgible for further paid rounds.
