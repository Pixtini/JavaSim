# Symbol transformations

Defines the rules around transformations.

**TRA-001 -** A posistion on the grid is targeted through some means, either by its location or by the current symbol within that position.
**TRA-002 -** That posistion becomes a target symbol.
**TRA-003 -** The symbol that was previously in that position ceases to exist in any capacity.
**TRA-004 -** Multiple posistions can be transformed at a time.
**TRA-005 -** Only one transformation can take place on a position at a time. Unless specified otherwise.
**TRA-005 -** Some symbols/positions may be excluded from being transformed. These do not get transformed under any circumstances unless there is a specific rule that allows for an "upgrade".

# Examples

Example of feature. A cluster game has a feature where at random all Low symbols are transformed to their Top counterparts. After reels drop, there is a cluster of 5 L1 symbols, and the feature triggers. All Lows becomes Tops. Lows cease to exist on the grid. The previous L1 cluster becomes a 5oak T1 cluster and pays as such. No L1 payout was awarded as those symbols dont exis after the transformation.
