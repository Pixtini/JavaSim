# Random draws

Define how random outcomes are selected and how their probabilities are specified.

**RAN-001 -** Outcomes are largely independant from one another unless specified otherwise. 
**RAN-002 -** Reels stops are decided uniformly and independant from one another.
**RAN-003 -** Weighted tables are used in abundance in majority of features. Weights are added and the result is always uniformly drawn with replacement. 
**RAN-004 -** There are times when outcomes may be ruled to be invalid, if so the weight is removed for that outcome before any draw is taken place.
**RAN-005 -** The above is default behaviour unless specificed otherwise by the GDD.
**RAN-006 -** Random selections can take place throughout a round.
