# Research method and skill improvements

Scope: 21 April–20 October 2026. Completed 20 October 2026. These notes describe the original research run. The separately requested [methodology review](methodology-review.md) records the subsequent omission investigation and supersedes the original coverage assessment.

## Steps and source selection

1. Read repository validation policy and the research skill's discovery, evidence and publishing references. Kept the earlier pilot artifacts intact.
2. Searched broadly, then opened original provider pages, monthly Eilers summaries, operator disclosures and current lobbies. Followed real source links rather than assuming guessed archive URLs exist.
3. Inspected May–September Europe, June–September US online/US land-based, August–September EMEA, Canada September, and BingBong August/September. Kept report month distinct from observation dates.
4. Checked official products/release archives for early-window and recent mechanics. Flagged inconsistencies rather than resolving them by guesswork.
5. Inspected original community discussions and assessed a promotional Reddit result. Did not compute sentiment percentages or count repeated quotes/regulars as independent people.
6. Added supplier financial filing metadata, regulator publication/measurement metadata, and SlotRank's own methodology as context outside a narrow game-release scan.
7. Created a cited report, claim ledger, source register and publication manifest; verified Notion readback. Validation covers documents/metadata/publication only.

## Demonstrated issues and useful improvements

- A report page's Similar Reports cards contain newer unrelated summaries. Extract only its own main description; otherwise facts can be misattributed to the wrong month.
- Europe panel sizes changed from89/18 to69/15; US from35to40 sites. Equal size later still does not prove constant membership. Maintain a panel/change log before trend calculations.
- NEW segment labels are not release dates: a Buffalo title is still categorized NEW in its twelfth month. Obtain release evidence separately.
- BingBong provides rare operator wagering shares but contains790/782-versus90 denominator contradictions. Preserve values as provisional claims and seek clarification rather than silently fixing the source.
- Official supplier pages can conflict internally: Zeus maximum dimensions; Inks volatility/evaluation metadata. Primary provenance does not eliminate the need to verify versions/help.
- Review-linked pre-release Reddit copy is not independent satisfaction evidence. Source dates and commercial purpose can disqualify seemingly useful posts.
- Bare keyword provider searches produced irrelevant/compromised search results. Prefer verified official hostnames, domain-filtered queries and links from original catalogs.
- Some guessed monthly archive URLs failed; official Nolimit root could not be fetched. Failed retrieval is not proof a source/title is absent.
- Dynamic counter values differ across page caches. Capture fetch time and source crawl freshness; do not synthesize historical player series from current snapshots.
- Do not equate a provider's launch/distribution growth or financial revenue with title turnover.

## Reusable source additions

The skill gains a concise source directory with original methodology and data routes: Eilers store and panel caveats; BingBong operator metrics with the denominator warning; SlotCatalog exposure methodology; Nasdaq issuer filings; UKGC series with period checks; official mechanics archives; and original forum routes with incentive checks.

The discovery guide links the directory and adds guidance for retrospective multi-month research. These are discovery routes, not an endorsed permanent ranking or a requirement to fetch every source on every run. Existing Notion destination and validation scope remain in force.

## Material limitations

No paid datasets, deposits, authenticated browsing, complete live rules audit, corporate ownership audit, constant-panel title history, representative multi-channel sentiment sample or complete global coverage. No operator contacts or recurring automation. No game or engine files changed for this task.
