# Research the slot market

Produce an evidence-based report that helps design future slot games for JavaSim. Browse the live web for each report; do not reuse remembered rankings as current facts. Respect applicable repository instructions when saving local artifacts. Follow the [root validation policy](../../../AGENTS.md): research, report publication, and non-executable skill edits require content, citation, format, link, and publication checks, not Java compilation, game tests, or simulations.

## Scope and discovery

Use the requested markets, period, providers and Notion destination. Unless specified otherwise, state these defaults: an international English-accessible scan, online releases/news from the last 90 days, established performers from the last 12 months, and the latest available land-based performance reports. Keep geography and observation dates explicit; this is a sampled market scan, not exhaustive global coverage.

Cover the online operators Stake, Roobet, Rainbet and Duel alongside other relevant online operators, and land-based titles/operators. Duel is the operator specified by the user; do not substitute Duelbits. Search outside the largest suppliers. Use [the discovery guide](references/discovery.md) to plan operator, provider, community and land-based coverage.

An ordinary report should aim for a useful shortlist of roughly 8–12 standout titles across established performers and emerging ideas, with online and land-based representation where evidence permits. Adapt to the request and available evidence rather than filling a quota. Explain material coverage gaps.

## Research and assessment

Read [evidence and sentiment rules](references/evidence.md) before comparing performance.

1. Build separate candidate streams for in-window releases and established/current performers. For priority operators, seek first-party analytics/insights before relying on industry panels or lobby proxies; record which populations and periods those routes cover. Include specialist and smaller providers rather than restricting discovery to Pragmatic Play, Nolimit City or Evolution-associated brands. Verify current studio/brand/group attribution.
2. Fetch original sources for material claims. Record publication/measurement/access dates, geography, metric definition, source type, and commercial interests. Search snippets, copied press releases and affiliate roundups are discovery aids rather than independent corroboration.
3. Separate measured turnover from GGR/win, rankings, active-player indicators, installed base and lobby placement. Rank comparable turnover observations only within the same defined cohort; label marketing claims and proxies. Where verified title-level turnover is unavailable, say so and report the best supported signals without inventing a top-turnover leaderboard.
4. Inspect standout mechanics and screen topology using official rules/help, demos or reliable gameplay evidence. Record fixed and changing reel heights, active cells, evaluation type, feature sequence, persistent state and bonus-buy differences. Distinguish new-to-market evidence from a familiar mechanic in a new theme; label novelty uncertain when historical support is missing.
5. Assess sentiment from distinct first-hand discussions and contrary evidence. Avoid equating losses with cheating, or dismissing all complaints because players lost. Identify specific design/technical concerns, version/RTP differences, incentives and sample limits. Do not equate affiliate praise, sponsored streams or selected big wins with representative satisfaction.
6. Reconcile the shortlist with prominent candidates in accessible turnover, bets/activity and historical-performance views. Record inclusion, exclusion or deferment reasons using the [candidate register](assets/candidate-disposition.csv) when useful. A leader with an unknown launch date still belongs in the established-performer stream; mechanics interest and easy source access should not silently displace performance priorities.
7. Synthesize performance evidence, sentiment, mechanics and provider coverage separately. Review priority-market coverage and source concentration before publication; repeated pages from one panel are temporal depth, not independent market coverage. State unresolved coverage explicitly. Translate findings into original design hypotheses and testable questions, not copied PAR values or claims of commercial certainty.
