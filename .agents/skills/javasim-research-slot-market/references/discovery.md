# Market discovery and coverage

Use these as search directions, not claims about current availability, ownership or performance. Verify current sites, regional access and provider identities during each run.

## Online operator scan

- Include the online operators Stake, Roobet, Rainbet and Duel. Treat Duel as a distinct named operator; do not substitute Duelbits. Record each hostname/region and whether the observed lobby is public, personalized, logged in, or promotion-driven.
- Inspect top/popular/new sections, game detail pages, public activity feeds and any disclosed counters. Record their labels and observation time exactly. A lobby position or recent-bets feed does not establish turnover.
- Seek public operator/platform analytics and insights pages as well as lobbies. For Stake, investigate [Stake Engine Insights](https://stake-engine.com/insights) and preserve its Engine cohort rather than calling it Stake's entire catalog. Compare relevant turnover and bet-count views; they may surface different leaders.
- Supplement with other accessible operators and markets to reduce crypto-casino selection bias. Operator-specific findings should remain operator-specific.
- Do not bypass geographic/access restrictions, make deposits or place wagers to collect evidence. Mark login-gated, dynamic, unavailable or unverifiable data and seek accessible corroboration.

## Search patterns and saturation

Keep a candidate disposition record linking discovered leaders to inclusion, exclusion or deferment reasons. Reconcile the final report against prominent candidates from relevant performance views before stopping; an interesting-mechanics shortlist alone does not satisfy high-turnover discovery. Separate current/established performers from new releases, so unknown launch dates do not remove relevant games.

Check coverage by priority operator/market and metric, not total source count. Record first-party routes investigated, actual population/time coverage and gaps. Many monthly pages from one publisher cannot substitute for a missing named-operator ecosystem.

When a dynamic page returns only a loading shell, try another permitted public retrieval route, such as its public data request or a supported rendered view, or record the unresolved access gap. Preserve request filters, retrieval time and raw/display units. Search-cache figures must retain their freshness and retrieval limitations; do not label them live.

For retrospective multi-month reports, maintain a month-by-market coverage table. Separate publication, measurement and release dates; record panel changes before comparing editions. A current counter or catalog cannot reconstruct historical player counts. An inaccessible monthly page is a coverage gap, not evidence that no report or release exists. Label uneven coverage and partial boundary months explicitly.

Use a discovery pass followed by targeted verification. Examples:

- operator + game + popular / players / bets / methodology
- title + official rules / feature / grid / release date
- studio + latest releases / unusual reels / persistent feature
- title + player discussion / bonus frequency / pacing / bugs
- land-based + month/year + slot performance + coin-in / win per unit / index
- mechanic name + earlier games / first release

Search counterevidence and older precedents for major claims. Include multilingual or regional material when useful and interpret translations cautiously. Stop when requested dimensions have credible coverage and remaining uncertainties are explicit; explain access-limited coverage instead of chasing inaccessible data indefinitely.

## Extracting useful game ideas

For each selected title identify the player-facing hook, actual state transitions and why the screen shape matters. Separate branded feature names from underlying mechanics. Examples of dimensions to inspect: expanding grids, uneven reels, independent reel windows, clusters, collection states, respins, cascades, multipliers, persistent symbols, linked progressions and alternative feature entry costs.

For JavaSim ideas describe the Basegame and randomly triggered Feature Game relationship, new state/results required, reuse candidates, mathematical risks, and deterministic tests. Do not force a respin/cascade into a Freegame model without flagging the mismatch. Inspiration is a proposal; commercial data and missing PAR values are not design specifications.
