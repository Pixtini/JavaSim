# Source directory for slot-market research

Routes verified 20 October 2026. Reopen current pages on each run. These are useful starting points, not an exhaustive source list, required reading list or endorsement of commercial claims.

## Performance and methodology

| Route | Useful evidence | Decision-changing limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [Stake Engine Insights](https://stake-engine.com/insights) | First-party title and creator rankings by turnover or bets, with recent/calendar/cumulative and launch-relative views | Engine-published cohort; entire Stake catalog and operator split are not established. Check self-reported accounting, cash/demo/promotion and buy treatment, period boundaries and freshness. The inspected ranking UI divides raw turnover by 1,000,000 for USD display; platform stats use a different scale. Reverify before conversion. |
| [Eilers & Krejcik report store](https://ekgamingllc.com/downloads/eilers-fantini-europe-online-game-performance-report---september-26) | Online and land-based monthly public summaries; follow links to the relevant dated edition | Full tables/methods may require a license. Read the edition's main description, not newer Similar Reports cards. Record panel, region, segment and index; NEW is not a global release date. |
| [BingBong September 2026](https://www.bingbong.de/news/post/beliebteste-online-slots-september-2026) and [August](https://www.bingbong.de/news/post/beliebteste-online-slots-august-2026) | Rare operator-disclosed real-money title-wagering shares, excluding bonus-money stakes | One German operator; no absolute handle/player totals or independent audit. Both pages contradict their played-title population with a90-game denominator row. Preserve provisional operator claims until clarified. |
| [SlotCatalog SlotRank methodology](https://slotcatalog.com/en/read/What-is-SlotRank) | Country-level lobby exposure; daily incognito observations weighted by estimated traffic | Explicitly supply-side visibility. Not wagers, revenue, unique players or player satisfaction. Separate any separately licensed real-money-rounds product from SlotRank. |
| [UKGC Industry Statistics](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/statistics-and-research/the-gambling-market/series/industry-statistics) | Official market context and links to releases | Compare measurement dates with the research window; publication can lag by months. Aggregate GGY cannot identify title turnover or player preference. |

Third-party platform launches are not all in-house studio games. Annual/H1/Q2 periods may only partly overlap the requested window. |

## Mechanics, release dates and attribution

- [ELK dated archive](https://www.elk-studios.com/news/): useful launch chronology and grid summaries. Several entries share one source. A grid/feature list is not complete rules.
- [4ThePlayer news](https://www.4theplayer.com/news/) and [official products](https://www.4theplayer.com/our-games/): separate stated full-release dates from CMS modified dates and future roadmaps. Verify dimension conflicts in version-specific help.
- [Hacksaw products](https://www.hacksawgaming.com/games/nitro-nights) and [monthly roundups](https://www.hacksawgaming.com/news/may-round-up): distinguish studio originals from named OpenRGS partners; RTP menus do not identify deployed operator versions.
- [Push Gaming](https://www.pushgaming.com/), [Peter & Sons](https://peterandsonsgames.com/), [Thunderkick](https://www.thunderkick.com/news/), [Relax Gaming](https://www.relax-gaming.com/news) and [AvatarUX mechanics](https://avatarux.com/game-mechanics/stickypop/): useful specialist-provider routes. Undated mechanics references can establish precedents without establishing a new release.
- [Aristocrat Phoenix Link](https://www.aristocratgaming.com/us/games/us-phoenix-link): family pages can expose variant differences; do not transfer sibling features into a ranked title.
- [Coreffect title in Paperclip catalog](https://paperclipgaming.com/games/coreffectinteractive-candy-swipe-matchwin): catalog attribution and basic topology; exclusivity/promotions and unknown interactive rules require qualification.

These examples broaden provider discovery rather than replace regional studios or local-language sources. Distinguish studio, distributor, product platform and corporate group. If ownership is not verified, say so; do not count reporting labels as independent groups.
