# Evidence, performance and player sentiment

## Keep measurement definitions intact

| Term | Meaning to establish | Do not substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Turnover / handle / coin-in | Total wagered in a defined period, currency and population; check whether recycled wins, bonus funds or bonus buys count | GGR, deposits, total payouts, jackpot size |
| GGR / casino win | Stakes minus payouts under the source's accounting basis | Turnover or player preference |
| Performance index / win per unit per day | Exact numerator, denominator, panel and normalization | Global turnover ranking |
| Unique players | Distinct players over a stated interval and population, if verified | Concurrent sessions, spins or accounts on the platform |
| Active players / playing now | Source-defined live count with timestamp; it may represent sessions or an opaque estimate | Monthly unique players or cumulative reach |
| Bet/spin count | Number of observed wagers/spins and observation window | Unique players or currency volume without stake data |
| Lobby rank / featured placement | Position in the observed lobby/category | Proven turnover, unpromoted organic demand |
| Installed base / occupancy | Machines installed or observed utilization with venue/time scope | Coin-in per machine or title-level profitability |

Keep online and land-based comparisons separate. Also separate current high performance, installed-base popularity, new-title momentum and unusual-design interest. Avoid combined worldwide leaderboards built from unrelated panels or proxies.

For every quantitative comparison capture measurement period, source date, region/operator/panel, exact title and version, mode, units, methodology and sample size if disclosed. Normalize only when input definitions support it. If original currency conversion is needed, retain original values and cite dated rates. Rankings can favor placement, promotions, exclusivity, cabinet distribution, high stakes or a small number of heavy users; explain these as possible confounders, not established causes.

## Evidence labels

Assess each important claim, rather than assigning one blanket rating to a whole source:

- **Measured:** disclosed metric with identifiable scope/methodology; note audit status and panel limitations.
- **Claimed:** operator/supplier statement without sufficient underlying method.
- **Observed proxy:** a lobby position, visible counter, sampled activity or comparable indirect signal.
- **Anecdotal:** individual experience, forum comment or unverified account.
- **Unknown:** needed evidence is unavailable.

State confidence with a reason. An official source is usually strongest for rules, but its sales claims still need scrutiny. Multiple articles repeating one press release are one underlying source. Do not invent data to fill tables or turn missing metrics into zero.

For first-party analytics, distinguish confidence that a response/rank was captured from confidence in its underlying accounting and population. Preserve source-defined time filters and raw units; verify display scaling rather than assuming API integers are dollars. Keep a platform's published-game cohort separate from an operator's full catalog. Unknown cash/demo, promotional-play, bonus-buy or operator-split treatment remains a limitation even when exact turnover and bet counts are returned.

## Sentiment analysis

1. Sample across more than one independent channel where possible; include recent and contrary views and consider patch/version/region differences. State channels, period and sampling approach.
2. Deduplicate copied posts, repeated claims by the same author, reposted wins and affiliate networks. Count distinct contributors only if actually established; engagement is not a population sample.
3. Classify concrete themes: feature pacing, clarity, perceived value, volatility tolerance, audiovisual experience, usability, technical faults, rule disclosure and loss-related fairness allegations.
4. Separate the feeling from the factual allegation. A losing streak does not prove manipulation, and advertised theoretical RTP does not refute a reproducible bug. For specific complaints seek versions, logs/screenshots, official responses and independent recurrence. A community thread alone cannot certify fairness or cheating.
5. Distinguish game complaints from casino withdrawal/KYC disputes, streaming presentation and configuration differences. RTP versions and bonus-buy pricing may change the experience.
6. Flag affiliate links, casino referrals, sponsorship, free/demo play and selected win clips. Treat positive and negative commercial incentives symmetrically; do not assume undisclosed independence.
7. Summarize agreement, disagreement, specificity and confidence. Avoid raw star averages or sentiment percentages unless the data collection supports them. Say "among the sampled discussions" rather than "players agree."

Seek independent support for major performance or sentiment conclusions. If only one interested source is available, retain the observation with a clear limitation rather than presenting it as corroborated.

## Citation and access discipline

Open original pages before relying on material claims; record both publication and underlying measurement dates. Cite near claims and in the evidence ledger. Respect quotation limits and summarize player comments without publishing unnecessary identifying details. A paywall, missing counter definition or access restriction is a report limitation, not permission to invent or bypass data.

No observed ranking proves that a mechanic caused performance. Design ideas are hypotheses requiring prototyping and mathematical validation.
