# Research rationale — Ember Forge

Based on the [corrected six-month report](../../../docs/research/2026-10-20-six-month/market-report.md), its [methodology review](../../../docs/research/2026-10-20-six-month/methodology-review.md), [claim ledger](../../../docs/research/2026-10-20-six-month/evidence-ledger.csv) and [candidate register](../../../docs/research/2026-10-20-six-month/candidate-disposition.csv), revision 2 dated 20 October 2026.

## Selection and limits

The report's Cloudforge case suggests a compact multiplier-led experience; its Waylanders Forge correction shows that relevant Engine performers must be considered even with unknown release dates. Neither proves progression causes turnover or player satisfaction. The sampled forum criticism of option overload supports testing a clear single-bonus design, not a population preference claim.

| Candidate direction | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Large collection board inspired by Pirots/Bjorn | Reject | Unnecessary topology and unsupported collection/cluster-family rules for this run |
| Multiple modifier/bonus tiers inspired by Waylanders and multi-pot games | Reject | Violates one-Freegame constraint and creates explanation burden |
| Simple line game with one visible Freegame multiplier | Select | Compatible with broad lines; one small state variable and familiar evaluation |

Original design proposal: Heat rises after a winning Freegame spin, benefiting subsequent spins. This is not represented as any referenced game's actual rules. Forge theming is generic; no proprietary assets or PAR values are copied.

## “Will the player actually care about this feature?”

| Addition | Visible benefit | Evidence status / complexity | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat after a winning free spin | Later wins are multiplied; current/next Heat is shown | Design hypothesis; one bounded integer, clearly defined order | Include as the single differentiator |
| Wild substitution | Familiar route to connecting line wins | Standard lined vocabulary; shared mathematics exists | Include |
| Retriggers / extra bonus mode / wheel | Longer sessions or novelty, with more rules and mathematical tails | No compelling need within this small prototype | Omit |
| Expanding reels, cascades, jackpots | More spectacle | Adds unsupported or unnecessary mechanics; distracts from Heat | Omit |

Six regular and two special symbols; two BG sets and one FG set reused across all three purchase modes. Research is inspiration, not balanced mathematical input. Product appeal and player comprehension still need human playtesting.
